## ComfyUI Music Production Toolkit

`lyricist.py`'s system prompt adapts song-writing conventions from the YuE2
system prompts in the ComfyUI Music Production Toolkit
(<https://github.com/jplenio/ComfyUI-MiniMax-Music-Production-Toolkit>):
style and lyrics sharing one section map, repeats written out, instrumental
sections kept tag-only, and nothing but singable words under a tag. Anything
ported from that project later (see `docs/toolkit-integration.md`) is
covered by the same notice.
